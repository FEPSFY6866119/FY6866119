Conference paper: Use of Artificial Intellegence in diagnosing mental health issues

Introduction

As the use of artificial intelligence makes waves in industries across the world, an area with huge potential in this aspect is the healthcare industry, more specifically in helping Psychiatrists diagnose and spot underlying mental health issues.
Spotting and diagnosing mental health issues can be a complex process because of there being no perfect physiological test to determine whether someone has a mental illness. The lack of an infallible process to diagnose mental illness leaves space 
for Artificial Intelligence to modernize our current processes.

How has AI been used in psychiatry before

Artificial Intelligence has been applied in psychology for a substantial amount of time. With the earliest application created by Joseph Weizenbaum in the late 1960s which was a program called ELIZA, a simple chatbot with limited answers that
imitated a psychotherapist using Rogerian therapy (Olawade, 2024). This style of therapy is one developed by Carl Rogers and was based on the notion that the client knows best for themselves and is also encouraged to lead the therapy process.
