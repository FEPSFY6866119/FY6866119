Conference paper: Use of Artificial Intellegence in diagnosing mental health issues

Introduction

As the use of artificial intelligence makes waves in industries across the world, an area with huge potential in this aspect is the healthcare industry, more specifically in helping Psychiatrists diagnose and spot underlying mental health issues. Spotting and diagnosing mental health issues can be a complex process because of there being no perfect physiological test to determine whether someone has a mental illness. The lack of an infallible process to diagnose mental illness leaves space for Artificial Intelligence to modernize our current processes.
